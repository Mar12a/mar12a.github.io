Hi, I’m

Artist & Engineer

I love learning, creating and doing cool things. I completed my BSc in maths and physics at Utrecht University, and I’m on my way to becoming an electrical / biomedical engineer at Eindhoven University of Technology. I love creating with others and previously studied at HKU. I now study at the Gerrit Rietveld Academie. I have been drawing for as long as I can remember. I move between these worlds and bring them together. Welcome to my world!

Explore my work in the portfolio, or contact me below. I’d love to hear from you!
